怎麼試算

  1. 第一次開啟時選一個手續費折數。之後可在試算頁上方的折數膠囊更改。
  2. 選股票或 ETF，再選多方或空方。
  3. 輸入買進價，點張數（1、2、5、10 張），按計算。
  4. 結果會寫漲或跌幾檔開始獲利，表格列出每一檔的價格和損益。

怎麼從對帳單反推折數

  1. 打開「反推」，點你的券商。元大這類單邊明細抄「價金」和「手續費」。
  2. 國泰這類買賣彙總改抄賣出價金、買進價金和手續費合計。不要填持有成本或損益。
  3. 算出唯一折數後，可按「將手續費設為 N 折」。確認後會變成你的折數。

折數和稅

折數只看價金和手續費，與現股、當沖、ETF 的證交稅無關。買賣彙總若列出證交稅，沿用試算頁目前的股票或 ETF 當沖稅率，可能和對帳單上的稅不同。

How to use TW Day Trade Calc. If you still need help, email the address below.

Calculate a trade

  1. The first time you open the app, pick a commission discount. You can change it later from the capsule at the top of the calculator.
  2. Choose Stock or ETF, then Long or Short.
  3. Enter the buy price, tap a lot size (1, 2, 5, or 10), then Calculate.
  4. The result shows how many ticks until you profit, and a table of price and P/L at each tick.

Reverse a discount from a statement

  1. Open Reverse and tap your broker. For a single-side statement such as Yuanta, copy proceeds and commission.
  2. For a combined statement such as Cathay, copy sell proceeds, buy proceeds, and the combined commission. Do not enter cost basis or P/L.
  3. When one discount matches, tap “Set fee to …”. After you confirm, it becomes your discount.

Discount and tax

The discount uses proceeds and commission only. It does not depend on regular, day-trade, or ETF tax. If the combined view shows transaction tax, it follows the calculator’s current stock or ETF day-trade rate, which may differ from the statement.
