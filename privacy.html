隱私權政策

介紹 隱私權 支援

本政策說明當沖計算機（以下稱「本 App」）如何處理您的資訊。使用本 App 即表示您理解下列內容。

試算在您的裝置上進行

買進價、張數、損益、券商折數與對帳單上抄下的價金、手續費，都在您的 iPhone 上計算與保存。這些內容不會上傳到開發者的伺服器。

本機設定

折數、券商名稱、是否看過引導，存在這支手機的本機設定。刪除 App 會一併清除。

當機報告（Firebase Crashlytics）

正式版使用 Firebase Crashlytics，只收集未捕捉的當機與除錯資訊，用來修穩定性。不會上傳您輸入的價格、股數或對帳單數字。

廣告（Google AdMob）

完成引導並算出各檔損益後，表格中的隨機一列可能顯示一則橫幅廣告。App 會先透過 Apple 的 App Tracking Transparency 詢問追蹤許可；若您拒絕，廣告請求將以非個人化方式處理。開發與測試版本使用 Google 測試廣告，不會用您的正式廣告識別碼。

我們不出售個人資料

我們不會出售您的個人資料。

最後更新：2026-10-24。政策隨 App 功能調整時可能更新，請以本頁最新內容為準。

This policy describes how TW Day Trade Calc (“the app”) handles information. By using the app, you acknowledge the practices below.

Calculations stay on your device

Prices, lot size, profit and loss, your fee discount, and the proceeds and commission you copy from a statement are calculated and stored on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to the developer’s servers.

On-device settings

Your discount, broker name, and whether you finished the intro are stored in on-device settings. Deleting the app removes them.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

The production app uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect uncaught crashes and debugging information so we can fix stability issues. Prices, share counts, and statement figures you type are not uploaded.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

After the intro, once the price ladder is shown, a banner may appear in a random row of that table. The app asks for tracking permission with Apple’s App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, ad requests are non-personalized. Development builds use Google’s test ads and do not use your production advertising identifier.

We do not sell personal data

We do not sell your personal information.

Last updated: 2026-10-24. This policy may change as the app changes. This page is the current version.
